Documentation writing samples

Hannah Lawrence-Brown

Five documents covering four content types. Four of them come from Pennsylvania's supplier registration and procurement systems (a project I most recently worked on with United States Digital Response). The fifth is written against Rippling's published App Shop integration documentation.

Tutorial
Quick start guide: registration and certification

Takes a small business from unregistered to registered, certified and finding contract opportunities. Conditional paths by business type, prerequisites before each step, time estimates per stage, and a full Spanish translation.

Reference
Supplier systems and identifier reference

The structural counterpart to the guide. Which system owns what, which identifiers move between them, what states a supplier record can hold, and what each certification status permits.

How-to guide
Setting up E-Alerts for your NAICS codes

One task end to end, including the part most procedural guides leave out: confirming it worked, and diagnosing it when it hasn't. Expected result after every step, plus a troubleshooting reference.

Analytical report
Economic impact of Pennsylvania's procurement process

The analysis the three documents above came out of. Written for decision-makers rather than practitioners, which makes it a different audience, a different structure and a different job from the guides.

Reference · Markdown source
Credential lifecycle and troubleshooting

Drafted for Rippling's App Shop Integrations documentation. Consolidates credential lifetimes, reuse rules and renewal paths into one lifecycle table, with a troubleshooting reference for the installation flow. Written in Markdown with frontmatter to match their docs-as-code setup.

Written from Rippling's published documentation rather than against a running integration. The structure and editorial choices are mine to defend; the technical claims would need checking by someone who has built on the API.
