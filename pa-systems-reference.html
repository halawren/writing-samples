About this reference

What this document covers, who it is for, and how it relates to the Quick Start Guide.

The Quick Start Guide is task-oriented. It walks a supplier through registration, certification and finding opportunities in the order those things have to happen.

This document is the reference counterpart. It does not walk through anything. It describes the structure a supplier is moving through: the separate systems involved, the identifiers that connect them, the statuses a supplier record can hold, and what each status makes available. Use it when you need to look something up, resolve a discrepancy, or understand why a step in the Quick Start Guide is ordered the way it is.

📖 Who this is for

  • Suppliers resolving a specific blocker rather than starting from scratch
  • Procurement and BISD staff answering supplier questions
  • Business assistance organisations advising multiple clients through the process

⚠️ Scope

  • Describes the supplier-facing behaviour of each system: what a supplier submits, receives and must carry forward
  • Does not describe backend data exchange between Commonwealth systems, which is not publicly documented
  • Timeframes are the published service levels, not guarantees

Identifiers

Five values connect a supplier across the systems above. Each is issued by a different authority.

Identifier reference
Identifier Issued by Obtained Required for
Federal Tax ID (EIN) IRS Before registration begins Supplier registration
SSN SSA Already held Supplier registration, for sole proprietors in place of an EIN
Vendor number Commonwealth, via the Supplier Portal By email, 1 to 3 business days after registration Certification, bidding, receiving payment
NAICS code US Census Bureau classification, self-selected Looked up at census.gov/naics Registration, opportunity matching, E-Alerts
Third-party certificate An approved external certifier 1 to 90 days, varies by certifier SDB and VBE verification only

💡 The vendor number is the join key

It is issued by one system and consumed by every other one. A supplier cannot certify, bid or be paid without it, which is why registration has to complete before anything else can start. If a supplier is blocked at any later stage, confirming the vendor number is the first thing to check.

✅ NAICS codes do more than describe the business

  • They are the matching key E-Alerts uses to decide which solicitations a supplier is notified about
  • A supplier with too few codes registered will silently receive fewer notifications
  • Codes can be updated at any time through the Supplier Portal
States

Supplier status model

A supplier record moves through a fixed sequence. Each status has one entry route and grants a specific set of capabilities.

Statuses, entry conditions and what each unlocks
Status Entered by Elapsed time Unlocks
Unregistered Initial state — Nothing. Solicitations can be viewed but not responded to.
Registered Completing Supplier Portal registration and receiving a vendor number 15 to 20 min plus 1 to 3 business days Bidding on open solicitations, receiving payment, applying for certification
Self-certified (SB) Self-certifying in PRISM against the size criteria Immediate on submission Small Business designation; required precondition for SDB and VBE
Verified (SDB) Holding a valid third-party diversity certificate and submitting it for PA verification Under 10 business days from a complete submission Small Diverse Business designation
Verified (VBE) Holding a valid veteran-specific third-party certificate and submitting it for PA verification Under 10 business days from a complete submission Veteran Business Enterprise designation

⚠️ SDB and VBE are additive, not alternative

  • Both require the supplier to also meet the Small Business size criteria
  • Neither can be reached directly. A supplier must be registered first, and must hold an external certificate before PA verification can begin
  • A supplier who applies for SDB verification without a third-party certificate in hand will be stopped, not queued
Comparison

Certification paths compared

All three are administered through PRISM Compliance, but they differ in prerequisite, evidence and turnaround.

SB, SDB and VBE side by side
Small Business (SB) Small Diverse Business (SDB) Veteran Business Enterprise (VBE)
Size criteria 100 or fewer FTE employees, or under $47M annual gross revenue Same as SB, required Same as SB, required
Ownership criteria None 51%+ owned by minorities, women, persons with disabilities, or LGBTQIA+ members 51%+ owned by veterans or service-disabled veterans
External certificate Not required Required, from an approved certifier Required, veteran-specific
Review type Self-certification PA verification of an existing certificate PA verification of an existing certificate
Turnaround Immediate Under 10 business days Under 10 business days
Where applied PRISM Compliance PRISM Compliance PRISM Compliance

A supplier may hold more than one designation. SDB and VBE are not mutually exclusive, and both sit on top of the Small Business size test rather than replacing it.

Solicitation types and platforms
Code Full name Award basis Platform
IFB Invitation for Bid Lowest responsive price eMarketplace
RFP Request for Proposal Overall value, not price alone JAGGAER

SFP Simplified for Purchase Streamlined, for smaller purchases JAGGAER

💡 COSTARS is a program, not a solicitation type

COSTARS is a cooperative purchasing program that lets local agencies, school districts and non-profits buy from contracts the Commonwealth has already awarded. A supplier reaches it by winning a Commonwealth contract first, not by responding to a COSTARS solicitation.

Analysis

What the supplier carries between systems

Each system issues something the next one requires. The supplier is responsible for moving it.

Manual handoffs in the supplier journey
From To What the supplier carries Delay introduced
IRS PA Supplier Portal EIN None, if already held
PA Supplier Portal PRISM Compliance Vendor number 1 to 3 business days
Third-party certifier PRISM Compliance Diversity or veteran certificate 1 to 90 days
PA Supplier Portal E-Alerts NAICS codes, re-entered None
PA Supplier Portal JAGGAER Supplier details, separate login created None

Reaching full SDB or VBE status requires accounts in at least four systems, two of which are outside the Commonwealth. Nothing in the sequence can be done in parallel: the vendor number cannot be requested before registration, and PA verification cannot begin before an external certificate exists.

⚠️ Where suppliers most often stall

  • Waiting on the vendor number. It arrives by email and is frequently filtered to spam, so the supplier believes registration failed
  • Applying for SDB or VBE too early. The third-party certificate is a precondition, not something PA obtains on the supplier's behalf
  • Registering too few NAICS codes. This produces no error, only fewer notifications, so it is rarely noticed
  • Assuming certification is required to bid. Registration alone is sufficient for open solicitations
Planning

Time to eligibility

Cumulative elapsed time from a standing start, using published service levels.

Elapsed time by path
Path Best case Worst case Governed by
Registration only 1 business day 3 business days Vendor number issuance
SB self-certification 1 business day 3 business days Vendor number issuance; certification itself is immediate
SDB or VBE, certificate already held ~11 business days ~13 business days Vendor number, then PA verification
SDB or VBE, no certificate yet ~12 days ~100 days Third-party certification, which dominates the total

✅ Planning guidance

  • A supplier with a specific solicitation deadline in mind should register immediately, since registration alone permits bidding
  • Certification can proceed in the background once the vendor number arrives
  • Any supplier intending to pursue SDB or VBE should begin third-party certification first, because it is the longest and least predictable stage

Glossary

Acronyms used across Commonwealth procurement systems and documentation.

BISD
Bureau of Diversity, Inclusion and Small Business Opportunities. Administers all certification within DGS.
COSTARS
Cooperative purchasing program allowing local agencies to buy from awarded Commonwealth contracts.
CVE
Center for Verification and Evaluation, US Dept. of Veterans Affairs. An approved veteran certifier.
DGS
Pennsylvania Department of General Services. Owns Commonwealth procurement.
DOBE
Disability-Owned Business Enterprise. An eligible diverse category for SDB.
EIN
Employer Identification Number, issued by the IRS. Required to register.
IFB
Invitation for Bid. Awarded on lowest responsive price. Posted to eMarketplace.
ITQ
Invitation to Qualify. Establishes a pre-qualified pool rather than awarding directly.
MBE
Minority-Owned Business Enterprise. An eligible diverse category for SDB.
NAICS
North American Industry Classification System. Codes identifying what a business supplies; used for opportunity matching.
NaVOBA
National Veteran-Owned Business Association. An approved veteran certifier.
NMSDC
National Minority Supplier Development Council. An approved diversity certifier.
P3
Public-private partnership. Procured through R3-RFQ.
PRISM Compliance
The portal through which BISD administers SB, SDB and VBE certification.
R3-RFQ
Request for Qualified Applicants. Used for P3 projects.
RFP
Request for Proposal. Evaluated on overall value rather than price alone.
SB
Small Business. Self-certified against Commonwealth size standards.
SDB
Small Diverse Business. Requires SB size criteria plus a third-party diversity certificate.
SDVOB
Service-Disabled Veteran-Owned Business. Covered under VBE.
SFP
Simplified for Purchase. Streamlined procurement for smaller purchases.
VBE
Veteran Business Enterprise. Requires SB size criteria plus a veteran-specific certificate.
VOB
Veteran-Owned Business. Covered under VBE.
WBE
Women-Owned Business Enterprise. An eligible diverse category for SDB.
WBENC
Women's Business Enterprise National Council. An approved diversity certifier.
